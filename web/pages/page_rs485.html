RS485 Slaves (up to 20)
RS485 is half-duplex — one request at a time across all registers on all slaves, always. Realistic interval budget grows with total register count (roughly 30-100 ms per register at 9600 baud when healthy) — avoid setting a slave's poll interval far below (its register count × ~50 ms), and raise the baud rate if your slaves support it to fit more registers in a shorter cycle.
